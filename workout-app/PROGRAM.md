# Pro Program — 4-Day Upper/Lower (Daily Undulating Periodization)

Built 2026-08-17 from current training research. This doc is the "why" behind the app's programming.

## Week Structure

| Day | Session | Bias |
|---|---|---|
| Mon | Upper A — Heavy | Strength: 4–8 reps @ 2–3 RIR, long rests |
| Tue | Lower A — Heavy Squat | Strength: 4–8 reps @ 2–3 RIR |
| Wed | Tempo Ride | Zone 3, 45–60 min |
| Thu | Upper B — Volume | Hypertrophy: 8–20 reps @ 0–2 RIR |
| Fri | Lower B — Hinge & Volume | Deadlift + hypertrophy accessories |
| Sat | Long Ride / Intervals | Zone 2 base (go easy if Friday was brutal) |
| Sun | Rest | Walk, mobility, sleep |

## The Rules

1. **Volume: ~12–16 hard sets per muscle per week.** Meta-analyses put the effective range at 10–20 with diminishing returns past ~15–24; this program lands chest 14, back 11 (+rear-delt work), delts 15, quads 13, hams/glutes ~12.
2. **Frequency: every muscle 2×/week** — what the upper/lower split exists for.
3. **Effort:** heavy compound work stops at 2–3 reps in reserve (going to failure adds nothing for strength and costs recovery); volume/isolation work pushes to 0–2 RIR (hypertrophy scales with proximity to failure).
4. **Rest:** 3–3.5 min on big barbell lifts, 2 min on secondary compounds, 60–90s on isolation. The app's timer enforces this per exercise.
5. **Progression — double progression:** work within the rep range; when you hit the top of the range on every set, add weight (+5 lb upper body, +10 lb lower body). The app flags "⬆ add weight today" automatically from your last session.
6. **Deload every 6th week** (flagged on the grid and in the workout screen): half the sets, same weight, stop everything at 4 RIR. Ride easy that week too.
7. **Cardio coexistence:** 2 rides/week doesn't blunt strength or muscle gains (interference only shows up at 3–4+ endurance sessions); cycling interferes less than running. Bike-to-gym commutes count as warm-up, not training stress.
